## For Colin

- Musical Theatre sets still say "number" for a song in a show ("the mood of a number", "evidence from specific numbers"). It is the course's own word and most parents know "musical number". I changed it only where it read unclearly ("the whole number", "the same number"). Say if you want "song" everywhere.
- Theatre Company 11 and 12: the Ministry asks students to connect with other theatre companies. I changed the next steps from "exchange ideas with a company" (an activity you would have to arrange) to "ask useful questions about how a company makes its work". The strengths still describe the connection itself.
