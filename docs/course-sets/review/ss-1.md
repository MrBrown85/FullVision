## For Colin to Decide

- **"Responsibility to respond" is no longer named.** The Ministry wording for 20th Century World History 12 and Human/Physical Geography 12 says "assess/determine whether we have a responsibility to respond". Every line that turned this into "who should act today, and how" failed the read-aloud test, so it was rewritten as weighing arguments and harms. If you want the clause back, the plainest version is "weigh arguments about whether people today have a duty to respond".
- **The remember/reconcile/respond clause is now judging, not prescribing.** For SS9/SS10/Francophone 11/Comparative Cultures 12 and B.C. First Peoples 12, the lines now say "explain why people disagree about how an event should be remembered" and "judge an effort at reconciliation by what First Nations have said they need". Nothing asks a student to suggest how to remember or repair a past wrong.
- **Two kept lines are close to the flagged pattern but describe understanding, not prescribing.** SS9: "explain why a community remembers a past event the way it does". SS10–12: "She explains how an apology or memorial affects the people it is meant for." Cut them if they still read oddly.
- **Genocide Studies 12** keeps "Her goal now is to explain why some people resisted while others stood by", and the home line "how people can stand up for others who are treated unfairly". Both are gentle, but this is the most sensitive course in the batch.
