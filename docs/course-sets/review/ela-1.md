# Review: ela-1

Sets read: 233 (59 groups, 1,398 sentences). Lines changed: 92. Checker: `clean (1398 sentences)`. Pre-review copy: `out-sets/ela-1.pre-review.json`.

## What Changed

- 48 next-step sentences opened "This term, …" (grades are year to date). Each now opens with an ordinary step opener.
- 16 Developing next steps opened "To go further, …", which reads as a stretch goal at the lowest next-step level. Each now opens "Her next step is to …" or similar.
- 19 lines named routines, materials or activities the Ministry does not name (writing circle, writing group, design log, reading log, word goal, "each day", "each week", slides, handout, "in the chat", "present on two societies").
- 4 home lines assumed a family routine or setting ("At dinner", "at your dinner table", "your family's languages").
- 1 step set one storytelling tradition as the norm ("differs from a typical plot").
- The rest are grammar, clarity and lint fixes. A few lines had two of these problems, so the counts overlap.

No set failed the read-aloud test outright: none asks a family to judge or probe, and the First Peoples, identity and protocol home lines are gentle and optional. No partition was clearly wrong, so no set was split or merged. `ela-information-use` is the approved sample and was left as is. Grade 8–9 sets already read younger than the 10–12 sets.
