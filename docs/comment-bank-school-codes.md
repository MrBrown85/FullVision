# The school’s comment bank: what FullVision adapts, and what it leaves

Written 2026-11-01 (lane E, `gen/bank-school`). Colin gave the school’s comment-code list (559 codes exported from the student information system, each with an id, text and category) and its ELL sheet (22 lines plus a fill-in template; the same 22 lines are codes 300–322 of the list), and asked whether they can be adapted for Generate. Every code is classified here, one line each, so the decisions can be reviewed. The source texts are data: nothing rejected is reproduced in the app.

## The three decisions

- **ADAPT**: rewritten into one or more bank sentences in `shared/comment-bank.js` (ids `sc…`, `src` "GHS <code>"), a Language Goals line (`lg-…`) or a support line (`sl-ghs-…`), keeping the sense of the school’s wording where it passes the four-audience checklist, so teachers recognise their own bank.
- **TEACHER LINE**: no gradebook fact can select it, so it becomes a line the teacher ticks (the support checklist, in the plan’s "In class, I will…" shape) or the class’s own typed closing sentence.
- **REJECT**: it fails the acceptability checklist (a label, praise of the person, an attitude or effort judgment, a comparison, a prediction, a home-life or designation reference, an order to the family) or is an admin notice, not a comment. Every rejection carries its reason.

Every sentence kept passed the four-audience checklist (`bank-acceptability.md`: Ministry, families, students, teachers), the vocabulary lint and `verify()`.

## Where the adapted codes went

- Grade frames `scr01–scr06` (GHS 26, 30, 34, 35, 43, 137, 192–196): the letter and the Letter Grades Order standing word, exact; C- has its own.
- Strengths frames `scn01–scn15`: what the student can do, from the skill table, with the school’s subject framing (assignments, sources, texts, labs, practical work, projects, career planning, skills and activities).
- Next steps `scg01–scg37` (scg07 and scg29 cut after the review of #601): one skill from the competency-to-skill table per frame (`when.nextSkill`), tagged to one kind of class.
- Support lines `sl-ghs-…` (GHS 36, 184, 188, 238, 401, 420, 475, 527, 611 and the Humanities teacher).
- Language Goals lines `lg-…` (GHS 302–321): a teacher-ticked list shown only when the student is assessed on language goals; each says what the student can do in English and one next step, never the designation.
