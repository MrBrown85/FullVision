**Subject:** Quizzes are coming to FullVision: what is stored about students

Hello,

The next FullVision update adds Quizzes. This email says what it adds and what it stores about students, as the privacy page promises before anything like it changes.

**What it is.** On an assessment, "They Answer a Quiz" opens a quiz you write yourself: questions, right answers, and the competency each question counts toward. Students answer on their own Student Page. You see the answers by student, by question and by competency, and FullVision suggests a level for each competency. Nothing counts until you confirm it. Nothing is sent to or imported from any other quiz service.

**What it stores.**

- Your questions and your right answers.
- For each student: their answer to each question, each try, when they started and when they sent it, and what you confirmed (which questions were right, and the points). For a written answer, your points and note.
- Answers save to the class as a student goes, so they are not lost if a phone is. With no connection, an answer waits in that browser tab until it saves, and is removed when it does or when the student logs out.

**Who sees it.** You see every student's answers. A student sees only their own. Classmates never see another student's answers or result. The right answers stay in a part of the database that only a returned quiz can read: they never reach a student's phone before you return that student's quiz and it has closed to them, and then only if you chose to show them. FullVision does not time a quiz or watch what a student does on their phone.

**Where it is kept, and for how long.** In Canada, with everything else: the same Montreal database, and the same Montreal service that carries students' cards. Quiz answers stay as long as the class exists and are deleted with it, 30 days after the class is deleted. A student's answers are deleted with the student.

**What you can do.** Quizzes are your choice, one assessment at a time; a class that never uses one stores nothing new. Download All My Data now has two quiz sheets, with your questions, your right answers and each student's answers. A class backup does not hold quizzes, and says so.

The privacy page has the same words: fullvision.ca/privacy. If you have a question, or would rather your class did not use Quizzes, reply to this email.

Colin
