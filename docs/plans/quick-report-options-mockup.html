★One ReportYour Direction

Custom is retired and its parts move into one panel, in switch cards (D1). No Quick Report / Custom switch and no name for the mode: the Progress Reports tab just opens on this. The former templates become Setups. On the Report lists what prints, top to bottom; drag a part's icon up or down to change where it prints. Everything else waits under Add a Part: switch one on and it slides into the list and onto the page. Click a part's name for its options; point to a part to find it on the page. The Parent / Guardian Response stays at the foot of the last page. Parts flow from page to page: a part that reaches the bottom of a page carries on at the top of the next (under its heading, marked continued), so no page ends with a gap. When the response slip lands on a back, the strip of the front behind it is kept blank, and each student's packet is an even number of sides so double-sided printing never puts two students on one sheet. Use Preview As to see a Grade 11 class in letter and percent.

Choose the workOpen Assessment List › Choose Assessments…: search, filter by category, quick picks (Counted Only, This Term, With Feedback), a switch per assessment.
Remove a row, a column or a partPoint to a row on the page and click the red −; point to a column heading for ×; point to a part for Hide. Hidden parts wait in the Add strip at the foot of the page. Undo is in the bar that appears.
Rename a headingDouble-click a heading on the page ("Assessments", "Teacher Comment") and type.
Edit the commentClick (or press Enter on) the Teacher Comment and type, at a readable size. Esc cancels. Click off and the changes show on the page with Save, Discard or Keep Editing. Unsaved changes never print. "Write Them" walks through students with no comment.
Move between students‹ › and the name menu above the pages; the menu flags who has no comment, unsaved changes or changes just for them.
Edit the questionsOpen Reflection: My Learning or My Habits › Edit Questions…, or Parent / Guardian Response › Edit Wording…. Change, add or remove any line; the page updates as you type.

Changes Apply To

Who a Change Applies To

The class

Choosing assessments and removing an assessment, competency, category or habit row applies to every student, unless Changes Apply To is set to the student. A banner above the pages says so while it is.

One student

Rows only about one student (an observation, a feedback card, a missing-work line, a Core Competency answer, an IEP goal) are removed for that student only, always. IEP Goals switch on for one student at a time. A blue dot, the student menu and Use Class Setting show and clear them; the printed copy keeps the list.

Nothing extra prints

Removed rows leave a dashed "2 removed · Show" chip on screen to bring them back; it never prints. Work left off the list prints no note, but the chip counts counted work apart from practice. Missing Work always lists missing pieces; removing one offers Excuse It Instead.

Questions

Edited reflection and Parent / Guardian Response wording is saved for that class only; a Setup and the teacher's other classes keep their own. It starts from today's wording, and Reset to Original Wording puts it back.

What Moves Over From Custom

Comes in as a switch

Letterhead, Overall and Competencies, Proficiency Legend, Assignment Grades (now Assessment List), Missing Work, Attendance, Competency Table, Progress in One Area, Next Steps, Teacher Comment, Learning Dispositions (now Work Habits), both Reflections, Ways to Support at Home, Parent / Guardian Response, Text Block (now Your Own Text).

Comes in as a part's options

Every option those blocks have today: Rows As Graded, Range, Organize By, When None, Period, lines for the comment, your own line for home. The Letterhead's choices and Name style.

Stays as it is

Paper and Front and Back in the toolbar. Print Reports saving a copy, and reprinting from it. Sent to Phones. The fit line. Leaving a part off one student (Custom's per-student list) becomes Cece Only.

Goes

Dragging and sizing blocks on the page's grid (order is set by dragging in the panel instead), side-by-side placement by hand (Attendance and Missing Work pair up when they're next to each other), the Blocks and Templates tabs, the Quick Report / Custom switch, and Self-Assessment and Goal (a student's own self-assessment and goal never print; a saved report that had it opens without it). Already-retired blocks stay retired.

Three panels reviewed the mockup above on their own: web and print designers (Krug, Nielsen, Norman, Wathan and Schoger, Wroblewski, Apple's guidelines, Tufte, a WCAG accessibility specialist); teachers and families (five BC teachers, a parent who reads English as an additional language, a Grade 8 student); and grading, policy, privacy and building (Guskey, O'Connor, Wiliam, the Ministry's reporting policy, the Privacy Commissioner's office and a district privacy officer, Fowler and Beck). Keep

Pointing at a part lights it on the page. Parts flow across pages with "(continued)". Glides of about 200 ms when anything moves. Levels as dots and a word. A running footer with the student's name on every page. Attendance and Missing Work pairing up. "4 students have none" on Teacher Comment. Undo that says "for Cece" or "for everyone". Arrow keys move a part.

Must Fix

  1. Missing Work disappears with the list. Teachers, O'Connor, Guskey, the Ministry Missing Work is built from the Assessment List, so taking a piece off the list (or choosing This Term) takes it off Missing Work too, while it still counts as a zero. Fix: Missing Work always reads the gradebook; removing a missing line offers "Excuse it instead".
  2. The tear-off slip cuts off the front. Tufte, Norman The Parent / Guardian Response sits at the foot of the back; printed double-sided, the family tears away the bottom of the front with it. Fix: keep the strip of the front behind the slip empty, or put the slip on the front's foot.
  3. Tracked changes could print. All three panels While an edit is unsaved, the page shows struck and underlined words, and printing saves the page as the family's copy. Fix: Print Reports asks to save or discard first, and the printed copy never carries screen marks. Saving goes through the Term Questionnaire's own save and says it clears Approved.
  4. Double-sided printing can mix students. Privacy officer Cece prints three sides; with Everyone on duplex, the next student's front lands on the back of her feedback page. Fix: pad every student's packet to an even number of sides.
  5. No way to move between students, and two look-alike switches. Teachers, Krug, Norman Whole Class / Cece Only (who a change applies to) looks like One Student / Everyone (what you see). Fix: a student menu with ‹ › above the pages that flags students with exceptions, no comment or unsaved changes; relabel the scope "Changes Apply To: Class / Cece" and say so above the pages while it's on Cece.
  6. Keyboard and screen reader users get lost. Accessibility Focus jumps to the top after every switch; the two pop-ups aren't real dialogs; Setup can't be reached by keyboard; Hide, × and − appear only on mouse hover, and the − is 11 px (24 px minimum). Fix: keep focus on the same control, real dialogs with Escape, controls shown at 24 px when a part has focus.
  7. Core Competency self-assessment. The Ministry, the VP The policy expects students' Core Competency self-assessment and goal setting in reporting; the report now has no way to include it. Wiliam and the privacy office back keeping the student's own words off paper. Middle ground they agree on: a blank Core Competency reflection and goal line that the student fills in by hand for the report.

Nice to Have

A visible grip on draggable rows. "Write Them" opens the comment editor on the first student with no comment, with "Next Without a Comment ›" and comment-bank drafts. The fit line names the fix ("Hide Proficiency Legend to fit one side, saves 27 sheets"). Point to a Missing Work line and its row in the Assessment List lights up. Where They Split

Saving the comment

Apple: save as you go, offer Undo. Norman and Nielsen: ask, because it goes home and into the Term Questionnaire. Middle: tracked changes stay, Save and Discard sit in the heading, no prompt on every click off.

Add strip on the paper

Krug and Wroblewski like it where the teacher is looking. Tufte and Wathan and Schoger want the page to show only what prints.

Fewer options or more

The Science teacher wants good defaults and to print and leave. The learning support teacher wants more per-student control.

Oversight

The VP wants the printed copy to record which counted work was left off. The veteran teacher calls that her professional judgment. A first-year teacher wants the school to lock required parts; the veteran refuses any lock.

Retiring Custom

Fowler: keep the old editor read-only for one reporting period. Beck: delete it once side-by-side tests match, so there's one code path.

What a student wants

Cece wants less on the page (no bold Missing, no "Lowest"). Her mother wants more: what to do, by when, and a meeting with an interpreter.

Nielsen Norman Group

Clarity · switch or checkbox

Use a switch when the change takes effect at once; a checkbox when the person still has to press Save or Print. The preview updates the moment a choice changes, so switches are the correct control here.

Supports: D1, D2.

Apple Human Interface Guidelines

Platform look

Switches in grouped lists are the settings pattern on iPhone and iPad and in macOS System Settings; checkboxes belong to Mac dialogs. FullVision already uses Apple's type, segmented controls and colours, which is why plain checkboxes look out of place.

Supports: D1.

Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger

Web design · Refactoring UI

Polish comes from hierarchy and spacing more than from a widget: group rows on a soft background instead of borders, push secondary text (counts) to grey, and use icons only where they help a person find their place.

Supports: D1's cards and grey counts. Warns: D3's tile grid is busy.

Steve Krug

Every Idea

The brainstorm in full. Option A has the ones marked In A; the rest could join it.

  • Range: Year to Date or This Term for the list. The grade stays year to date either way (DECISIONS, Reporting periods 2). In A
  • Choose Assessments: untick practice, excused or not-yet-due work. Counted work stays ticked. In A
  • Click a row on the sheet to leave it out; the same rule applies, and a line under the list says how many aren't listed. In A
  • Group by Category (or by Competency in a proficiency class), with a subtotal under each group. In A
  • Columns as chips: #, Date, Category, Score, Level, Status. Percent appears in a Letter or Both class. Replaces "Assignment Numbers". In A
  • Course Grade on or off, for a check-in that should start a conversation, not end it. In A
  • Missing Work by name and date, not "Missing Assignments: 0". In A
  • Competencies (As Graded) and Work Habits from the Term Questionnaire, kept apart from the grade. In A
  • Goals and Reflections and Core Competency Self-Assessment on the Feedback Pages. In A
  • Feedback First: print the feedback before the grade sheet. In A
  • A count beside every option for this student; a zero greys the option without disabling it. In A
  • Pages for the class in the fit line ("1 sheet + 1 feedback page for Cece · 54 pages for the class"). In A
  • Point to an option, its part of the sheet lights up. In A
  • Cece Only: an exception for one student, shown with a blue dot on the option and on her card in the student list; "Use Class Setting" clears it. In A
  • Setups: Term Report, Parent Conference, Check-In, Missing Work, and the teacher's own. Saved per class, offered in the next class too.
  • Only work with feedback on the Feedback Pages: skip pieces with no rubric and no comment.
  • Strengths and Next Steps pulled from the Term Questionnaire's At a Glance (strongest and next-step competency).
  • Attendance totals under the list, if the school's report needs them.
  • Missing comment warning before printing, like the Custom report's issue dialog: "4 students have no Term Comment. Write them now."
  • Send to Phones uses the same choices, so what prints and what the student reads match.

The Council

My readings of each member's published views, applied to this panel. They have not seen FullVision. For privacy, the real Commissioner's office and the district privacy officer should be asked.

Thomas Guskey

Grading and reporting

Report product, process and progress separately. A parent should see the grade, the work habits and the growth side by side, not blended.

Supports: Work Habits as its own block under the list; Competencies; a range choice.

Ken O'Connor

Grading and reporting

A grade must be explainable from the evidence behind it. Practice shouldn't count, and needn't be shown; work that counts should never be hidden.

Supports: leaving out uncounted work. Opposes: hiding counted work, even for one student.

Dylan Wiliam

Formative assessment

When a grade sits beside a comment, students read the grade and skip the comment. Lead with feedback; hold the grade back when the point is improvement.

Supports: Feedback First; Course Grade off for check-ins; Goals and Reflections.

BC K-12 Student Reporting Policy

Ministry of Education and Child Care

Written updates use the proficiency scale (K–9) or letter grade and percentage (10–12), with descriptive feedback. Students self-assess Core Competencies and set goals each year.

Supports: Core Competency Self-Assessment and Goals as options; Level column follows the class's grading style.

Steve Krug

Clarity

Every extra tick is a question the teacher has to answer. Put options in the order the teacher already thinks in, give sensible defaults, and let most people never open them.

Supports: A's paper order; B's setups. Warns: A at full length is a lot; keep defaults right.

Jakob Nielsen

Clarity

Show the result of a choice right away, prefer recognition to recall, and give an easy way back.

Supports: counts, page cost, hover-to-highlight, "Use Class Setting". Warns: a Whole Class / Cece Only mode is easy to forget you're in.

BC Privacy Commissioner · District Privacy Officer

Student privacy (FIPPA)

Share only what the reader needs. Private observations already never print. A per-student exception is a privacy tool: it lets a teacher leave a sensitive note off one report.

Supports: Cece Only; Observations off by default. Asks: does a self-assessment the student wrote for the teacher go home without the student knowing?

Where they splitHiding work. Teacher control (Krug, the privacy view) says let the teacher choose any row; grade integrity (O'Connor, Guskey) says counted work must print so the grade can be explained. A takes O'Connor's side. Second: the Cece Only mode. Privacy wants it; Nielsen warns about modes. A answers with the blue dot on the option and on her student card.

Decisions Owed

None open. Every decision for this panel is made.

Decided (Nov 12): Custom is retired and its parts fold into one panel; switch cards (D1); the templates become Setups; no mode switch or "Quick Report" name; a part's place is set by dragging its icon up or down in the panel; teachers choose which assessments print and can remove any row; no note prints when counted work is left off the list; teachers can reword the student reflection questions and the Parent / Guardian Response, saved for that class only, with Reset to Original Wording (this replaces Report forms 5 in DECISIONS ("the Parent / Guardian Response and the student reflection keep their wording") for a teacher's own edits; today's wording stays the default).; a teacher's saved Custom report opens as a Setup with the same parts on, in the same order, its saved form kept untouched, and printed copies reprint as they went home; a student's Core Competency self-assessment and goal may print, in the student's own words or as a blank form the student fills in, and the teacher never rates a student's Core Competencies (Nov 12, replacing "never on paper"); per-student exceptions replace Report forms 1 ("no per-student exceptions") and are kept with the printed copy
