# Term Questionnaire, rebuilt around the comment: build plan

Written 2026-10-28 from the design canvas "Term Questionnaire Mockup" (https://claude.ai/artifact/NY2EwwiZ8Ug6GiPqj4hHnV, version 19: the Cece, Jasper, No Observations and 1366 × 768 laptop artboards, and "Questions Come From the Report"). Decision in `DECISIONS.md`, Term Questionnaire 14. This is the working plan: what the page becomes, where its data lives, the PRs in order, and what each must prove. It finishes what `report-forms-plan.md` Phase 8 left out ("the Term Questionnaire's comment builder: its own canvas and plan").

## What the page becomes

One student at a time, the whole picture on one screen, and the teacher leaves with the comment. Three columns under the toolbar and the student strip:

- **The roster rail** (left, 260 px): search, All · Not Ready, one row per student with "Needs Approval" under the name until the comment is approved, then a tick.
- **The student strip** (full width): initials, name, the About Me line (how to say the name, pronouns, student number, languages, how they learn best), the goal tile (the course goal's text with the latest Goal Check-In status in its label; "Not set yet"), the attendance tile (Classes Absent, Times Late, totals to date from MyEd, the change since the last report as today). On a laptop a BC Requirements button sits here too.
- **What FullVision Has** (middle-left, scrolls on its own): Growth (every score this term per competency, oldest to newest, as dots on three rows by level, Start → Now in figures from `formatLevel`; Handed In, Late, Missing), Observations (this term; a note already quoted in the comment says "In the comment"; none: "The comment works without them"), the student's Voice (About Me answers, the latest check-in, the private note marked Private · Never Printed, unanswered forms as Not Answered).
- **The Comment Bank** (middle-right, scrolls on its own): four parts in BC's order, 1 Work Habits, 2 Where ⟨First⟩ Is Now, 3 Where ⟨First⟩ Needs to Go, 4 How ⟨First⟩ Will Get There. Part 1 opens with the six habit scales. Parts 2 and 3 carry choosers (Note to quote; Focus Area; I Can). Under each part, sentence rows grouped by source, two per group until Show All, each with Add; an added sentence shows ✓ Added and stays shown. A footer counts the sentences waiting on ratings or data the student does not have yet.
- **Teacher Comment** (right, 400 px, pinned; never scrolls away): tabs for this report and each earlier report (earlier ones read only, "Went home in ⟨report⟩"), the line meter against the form's box, a plain-text box that prints as one paragraph, Edit any word, Clear, Copy, Approve Comment. Under it the BC Written Learning Update card: the six requirements with a tick or a dot and where each is met, Show All adds "Also on This Report", Left for You, Open in Progress Reports.
- **The bottom bar**: ‹ Previous, "n of N · ⌘ Return saves and moves on · ↑ ↓ change student", Save and Next ›.
- **The toolbar** keeps the Term Questionnaire · Progress Reports tabs, the student stepper, the report picker, and gains "n of N Ready", Questions (the sheet "Where Each Block Gets Its Content") and Save and Next.

Below 1400 px the evidence column becomes tabs (Growth · Observations · Voice), the comment box is shorter, and the BC card opens from the strip's button.

Names stay the live ones: reports are "Report 1 … Report 6" (or the preset's label), not "Term 2"; levels and changes print through `calc.js` (`formatLevel`, `levelChangeAs`), never `toFixed` on the page.

## What leaves

The nine disposition pills, Quick Profile (Work Habits, Participation, Traits), the Strengths and Growth tag pickers, the assignment and observation Mention toggles, Mention in Comment for attendance (its two sentences are in the bank), the Academic Snapshot, the rich-text toolbar, and Generate with `tqAutoNarrative` (about 970 lines; its usable sentences are the bank). The columns those wrote (`work_habits_rating`, `participation_rating`, `social_traits`, the strength, growth, mention tables) stay in the database and in the phone's card widgets; nothing deletes them, nothing writes them.

## Data

- **The comment** stays in `term_rating.narrative_html`, now plain text with paragraph breaks as newlines. On read, an old HTML comment becomes text (tags stripped, block ends become newlines). The Teacher Comment block prints paragraphs.
- **The six habits** are new: `term_rating.habits jsonb not null default '{}'`, keys `completes`, `ready`, `others`, `part`, `feedback`, `devices`, values 1 Rarely · 2 Sometimes · 3 Usually · 4 Consistently, 5 Not Seen, absent = not rated. Completes Work starts from the assignment record when unrated (all in, none late or missing → Consistently; more than two missing → Rarely; any missing or more than two late → Sometimes; else Usually) and the teacher's pick overrides it. The nine `dims` the page rated until now were never sent to the server (`upsertTermRating` sends `dims`, the wire map knows `dimensions`, and the class bundle never read them back), so they were per-device only; they stop being rated and the Learning Dispositions block reads `habits` instead.
- **Approval** is new: `term_rating.approved_at timestamptz`. Approve Comment sets it; editing the comment after approval clears it. Ready = approved. Nothing gates printing on it.
- **Attendance** stays `absences` and `lates`, totals to date, with `getTermAttendance` for the change since the last report.
- `save_term_rating` accepts `habits` (whole map) and `approved` (true sets `approved_at = now()`, false clears it); `get_class_bundle.term_ratings` returns `habits` and `approved_at`; the client wire map, `_applyBundleTermRatings` and the audit rows carry them. One migration, `migrations/20261028_term_rating_habits_approval.sql`, tested on the scratch Postgres (`migrations/local-test/`, port 54331) and shown to Colin before it touches `gradebook-prod` (rule 1). CI's live-DB drift check fails until it is applied, so PR 2 cannot merge before Colin's OK; the UI PRs stack on it but run in Demo Mode without it.
- **The bank** is data in `shared/comment-bank.js` (`COMMENT_BANK`: `parts`, `sentences`; the canvas's `project/comment-bank.json`, 100 sentences, with its `about` and `cut` notes kept as a comment). Slots: `{name} {course} {He} {he} {him} {His} {his} {himself}`, `[singular|plural]` after a pronoun, and data slots (`standing`, `strengths`, `growthAreas`, `rise`, `fellCount`, `assignment`, `level`, `assignment2`, `level2`, `strongWorks`, `weakWorks`, `evidence`, `note`, `when`, `concernNote`, `concernWhen`, `selfGood`, `selfHard`, `selfStrong`, `selfLow`, `goal`, `plan`, `focus`, `nextStep`, `seeWork`, `aboutWork`, `strengthHabits`, `growthHabits`, `strengthHabitNames`, `growthHabitNames`, `absent`, `late`, `letterStanding`, `even`). A sentence is offered only when every slot it needs has a value and its `when` (habit levels, or the term's picture) matches. The engine (`teacher/comment-bank.js`, pure: context from live data, `fits`, `fill`, grouping, Show All, the "starts like your last sentence" note, and "used for 3 or more classmates this term" by matching each classmate's comment against the sentence with its slots as wildcards) has unit tests; nothing in it reads the DOM.
- **Evidence** comes from what exists: `getTagScores` and `ReportBlocks._sectionStartNow` for Growth; `getStudentQuickObs` for Observations; `StudentVoice.entries` / `profile` for Voice and the private note (`teacher_only`); `getGoals` and the check-in's `goal_status` for the goal tile; `ReportBlocks.focusArea` and the Next Steps block's I Can source for the choosers; the Missing Work block's rule for Handed In · Late · Missing.
- **The BC card** reads the class's form (`ReportSheets.formFor(getReportConfig(cid))`): Proficiency in Each Area is met when the Proficiency block is on the form; Work Habits, Strengths and Growth, Next Steps, Ways to Support when the comment holds a sentence from that part (or the matching block is on the form); Self-Reflection and Goals when the student answered or the Self-Assessment and Goal block is on. "Also on This Report" lists the form's other blocks with where each gets its content.
- **What FullVision Has** on/off (Growth, Observations, Student Voice, Assignments) is a per-device choice in `localStorage` (`gb-tq-cards-<cid>`), as the paper choice is.

## Process

As `report-forms-plan.md`: decide first (the DECISIONS entry lands with this plan), one PR per change on a `claude/<slug>` branch, stacked in order, CI green (vitest, prettier, the Playwright build) before a squash merge, Netlify deploys `main`. Every PR works in Demo Mode. Grade display changes none; the migration needs Colin's review and OK (rules 1 and 2). Title Case for labels and buttons; plain sentences for hints. No sentence is ever written by a model: the bank is the bank.
