# Where things stand

What is built, what is released, and what is not built yet. Updated in the same PR as the change it describes; after a release merges, the next PR to `main` updates the release line. Until 2026-11-26 this list was the "Current state" section of `CLAUDE.md` (older plans still call it that). Open work is in `ROADMAP.md`; decisions and their reasons are in `DECISIONS.md`.

## As of 2026-11-25

- Grading compliance with Schoology/Teams (`docs/grading-compliance-plan.md`): Phase 1 and Phase 2 are implemented and live.
- Course soft-delete with 30-day purge is live. Removing a student withdraws them (`migrations/20261019_withdraw_students.sql`); permanent delete is a second step from the Withdrawn list in Class Settings.
- Reports: Write Comments (the Term Questionnaire until Nov 26; its address is still `/reports?tab=questionnaire`) with its Comment Bank and Generate, and Progress Reports on One Report (one list of parts, for every teacher, since Nov 13). Students answer questionnaires on the student page's Reflections tab (`s/`); phone reports are no longer on it (Nov 14).
- Grades display only through `calc.js` and its helpers in `constants.js` (`formatLevel`, `scoreLevelText`, `BC_LETTER_BANDS`, `PROF_LABELS`); the grade-format-guard in `tests/grade.test.js` enforces it. Text goes into HTML only through `esc` in `shared/html.js`; `npm run audit:html` fails on a new unproven innerHTML site.
- The curriculum bank was rebuilt from the Ministry's files (Nov 2). New classes import from it through the New Class wizard; older classes change only when a teacher runs Re-link. Folders Count as One and Each Course Counts Equally are Grading tab switches.
- The student page build (`docs/plans/student-page-build-plan.md`) is built on `main` behind a per-class build switch. Not built: S15 (waits for Colin), S16 (paused), S17 (not in this build) and S18-F2 (waits for Colin). The last release merged into `production` was PR #1915 ("Release: Nov 27, 2026, second (+ the Dashboard's glance row, Next Report)") at 08:56 UTC on 2026-11-27, carrying everything on `main` up to #1914. Anything merged to `main` after it is not released.
- Reflect (`docs/plans/reflect-plan.md`): students reach reflections (the questionnaires) from the Reflect tab after their card sign-in, with no slips; teachers turn each one on in Class Settings › Reflect, build their own there, and read See Answers. Built on `main`; its database change (`migrations/2026111620_reflect_from_the_card.sql`) was applied Nov 16.
- The S19 cleanup follows `docs/plans/handoff-review-plan.md`. Its overnight run and the Nov 13 alignment work (`docs/plans/alignment-plan.md`) are merged and released.
- The Inbox was redrawn (`docs/plans/inbox-redesign-plan.md`): To Grade stacks, one per assessment with work waiting, and Roll Call, with the grader carrying on from one assessment into the next. It is on on `main` since I5 (Nov 24); the old views (By Assessment, Side by Side, Flip Through) are deleted. Released Nov 24 (#1724).
- The Dashboard's middle row (an Inbox and one Competencies tile, `docs/plans/dashboard-inbox-plan.md`) is on by default and released.
- Bulletins stage 1 (`docs/plans/bulletins-plan.md`) is on on `main` since B1g (Nov 18): the teacher's tab, the Dashboard tile, the phone's fourth tab and the student's first tab. Released Nov 18 (#1607).
- Quizzes (`docs/plans/quizzes-plan.md`), answered on the student page and confirmed by the teacher before anything counts, are on on `main` since Q6 (Nov 24): the Quiz card on the assessment form, the student's quiz and returned view, Results and Confirm, and a quiz that goes with its copy. Released Nov 24 (#1690); Q8 (the two surprises) is not built. Unfinished work stays behind the switches in `shared/build-flags.js`. Still Colin's: his OK on each migration, each release, and the district privacy officer's view of it (Colin waived the check before merge and the email to teachers on Nov 24; the draft is kept unsent in `docs/emails/2026-11-24-quizzes.md`).
- The phone's new look (`docs/plans/phone-look-plan.md`, DECISIONS "The phone's new look") is the phone's only layout since Nov 25, drawn natively: the grader and Grade list (`grade.css`, `grade-look.js`, `grade-reader.js`), the Students card and profile (`student-card.js`, `student-detail.js`, `students.css`), Observe, Bulletins and the top bar (`tabs.css`, `MC.tabBar`). Browser tests reach a student through the class list (`e2e/phone-jump.js`). Not built: Remind These 8, the squared-up board photo, a card made on the spot; the help pictures still show the old screens.

Its database change (`migrations/2026112601_rubric_comment_bank.sql`) was applied Nov 26. Released Nov 25 (#1811). Not built: the phone grader, the BC library "Curriculum" line.
