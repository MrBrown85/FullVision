Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FullVision is a gradebook and learning-profile tool for teachers in British Columbia. This page explains what information FullVision stores, where it is kept, who can see it, and how it is deleted.

Who runs FullVision

FullVision is built and run by Colin Brown, a teacher in British Columbia. It is an independent tool. It is not run by a school district or the Ministry of Education and Child Care. Questions about privacy go to colin.brown04@gmail.com.

Who uses it

Only teachers sign in. Students and parents do not have accounts, and FullVision never contacts them. Teachers enter student information themselves, so each teacher decides what goes in. Before entering student information, a teacher should confirm that their school district allows it.

What FullVision stores

About Information
Teachers Email address, display name, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and display settings. The sign-in service also records the time and IP address of each sign-in.
Students Name, preferred name, pronouns, student number, email address, date of birth, and learning designations (such as an IEP). Only a first name is required; the teacher chooses whether to add the rest.
Learning records Scores, proficiency levels and comments on assignments; observations; notes; goals and reflections; attendance; term ratings and report-card comments; and flags the teacher sets.
Classes Class names, competencies, assignments, rubrics and categories the teacher creates.
Change history When a score or term rating changes, the old and new values and the time of the change.

FullVision does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It does not sell, rent or share information for marketing. It does not send student information to artificial-intelligence services.

Where it is stored

  • The database is in Canada. All teacher and student information is stored by Supabase in its Montreal data centre (AWS ca-central-1).
  • Web pages are delivered by Netlify, which serves them from data centres around the world. Netlify handles page requests, including your IP address, but does not receive the information in your gradebook.
  • Error reports go to Sentry, which stores them in Germany. When the app hits an error, it sends the error message, where in the code it happened, the page, and the browser type. Before a report is sent, FullVision removes the app's log messages, form contents and the part of the page address that identifies a student, and masks email addresses, so reports do not carry student information.
  • Your own device. FullVision keeps a copy of your classes in your browser so it works offline, along with changes waiting to be saved. Signing out removes this copy. On a shared computer, always sign out.

Who can see it

  • You. Each teacher sees only their own classes and students. The database enforces this on every request; signed-in teachers cannot read another teacher's data, and people who are not signed in cannot read or run anything.
  • The operator. Colin Brown has administrator access to the database. He uses it only to keep FullVision running and to fix problems, such as a save that failed, and does not look at student information for any other reason.
  • Service providers. Supabase, Netlify and Sentry run the systems described above. They process information only to provide their service to FullVision.

FullVision does not give information to anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Information stays until the teacher deletes it.
  • Deleting a class hides it right away and permanently deletes it, with its students' records, after 30 days. Until then, it can be restored by contacting the operator.
  • Deleting your account (Delete Account at the bottom of the Advanced menu on the Assignments page) does the same for everything you stored: it is permanently deleted after 30 days. Signing back in during that time lets you cancel the deletion.
  • A student who no longer belongs to any class is deleted along with that class.
  • Change history for scores and term ratings is kept for 2 years, or until its class is deleted.
  • Backups. Supabase makes a daily backup of the database and keeps each one for 7 days. A deleted item can remain in backups for up to 7 days after it is deleted.

How it is protected

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption.
  • Supabase encrypts the stored data and backups.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes and are never visible to the operator.
  • Database rules restrict every request to the signed-in teacher's own data.
  • The pages only run scripts from FullVision itself and from Sentry's error-reporting service.

If something goes wrong

If information in FullVision is lost, or seen by someone who should not see it, the operator will tell the affected teachers as soon as possible and say what happened, what information was involved, and what is being done. Teachers can then inform their school or district as their own policies require.

Your choices

  • Teachers can view, correct and delete their information and their students' information at any time in the app, and can delete their account.
  • Students and parents who want to see, correct or remove information should ask the teacher or school, who control the records. The operator will help a teacher respond to a request.
  • For any other privacy question or request, email colin.brown04@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Changes that affect how student information is stored or shared will be sent to teachers by email before they take effect.
