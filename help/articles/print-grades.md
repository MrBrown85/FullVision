## Steps

1. Open the **Gradebook** and choose **Print Grades**.
2. Set what the sheet holds:

- **Term**: **All Time**, one term to date, or **Custom Dates**.
- **Assessments**: **All Assessments**, **Pick Assessments**, or, when Gradebook filters are on, **Shown in the Gradebook**.
- **Scores**: **Proficiency**, **Percentages** or **Points**.

3. Set how students are listed with **Show As** (Pupil # and Name, Name Only or Pupil # Only) and **Sort By**.
4. Set the page: **Final Grade** adds the overall grade, **Class Average Row** adds an average under each column, and **Late Marks** prints an L after late work. Choose the **Paper** and **Text Size**.

![The Print Grades window with the first page pictured at the left](img/print-grades.jpg 'The Print Grades window. The picture and page count update with each choice.')

5. Choose **Print**. The browser's print window opens.

The preview at the left shows the first page, with the number of pages, students and columns below it. The choices are kept for next time.

## Blank Sheets

Turn on **Blank Gradebook** to print the same grid with every score box empty. Turn on **Blank Attendance** to print school days across the top in place of assessments, Monday to Friday from the Monday of the current week.

## Sheets for One Assessment

The grader prints a sheet per student for one assessment, to hand back with the work. **Print Rubrics** prints the graded rubric for rubric and level work. **Print Scores** prints the points or Complete/Incomplete mark for points work.
