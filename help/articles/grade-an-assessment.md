## Score With Levels

1. Open **Assignments** and select the assessment. It opens with a row for each student and a line for each competency.
2. Select **Emerging**, **Developing**, **Proficient** or **Extending** on each line. Hover over a level to see its wording.
3. To add a minus or plus, choose − or + on the selected level.
4. To clear a score, select the chosen level again. **Undo** on the message puts it back.

![An assessment open with two students scored on Data Recording and Unit Conversions](img/grade-an-assessment.jpg 'An assessment scored on two competencies. Set Everyone To is the top row.')

## Score the Whole Class

In the **Set Everyone To** row, choose a level, then confirm in the dialog. Only scores not yet entered are set. Students marked Missing or Excused are left out.

## Mark Missing, Excused or Late

1. Beside the student's name, choose **Missing**, **Excused** or **Late**. Choose it again to remove it.
2. Missing and Excused apply only to a student with no score or comment on the work. Clear those first.

Missing work counts as 0 in the course % and is left out of competency proficiency. Excused work is left out of both. Late work is scored as usual.

## Add a Comment

Type in the **Add an overall comment** field under the student's scores. The comment saves as it is typed.

## Points and Complete/Incomplete

For a points assessment, type each student's score in the box beside the name. For a Complete/Incomplete assessment, choose **Complete** or **Incomplete**. **Set Unmarked to Missing** marks every student without a score as Missing. **Set Unmarked to Complete** marks every student without a score Complete. On a points assessment, the button beside it, such as **Set Unmarked to** 10 / 10, gives every student without a score full marks.
