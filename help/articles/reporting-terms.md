## Make Your Own Dates

Any teacher can set their own reporting periods, in Surrey or not, without picking a school.

1. Open **Class Settings** from the Dashboard, select the class, then open the **Reporting Terms** tab.
2. Choose **Create New**, beside **Dates From**. **Your Own Dates** opens with a copy of the class's dates.
3. To start from something else, choose under **Start With**: **4 Terms**, **3 Terms**, **2 Semesters** or **Blank**. **This Class’s Dates** goes back to the copy.
4. Name each term and give it a start date and an end date. Choose **+ Add Term** to add a term, up to six, or ✕ to remove one. The bar under the terms shows each one across the school year.
5. To give your other classes the same terms, choose them under **Also Use In**.
6. Choose **Save Terms**. **Cancel** keeps the saved dates.

Your other classes' own dates are listed under **Dates From**, under Your Classes. Choose one to see its dates, then **Use These Dates**.

## Use a School's Dates

1. On the **Reporting Terms** tab, choose the school under **Dates From**. Its term dates are listed under the menu.
2. Choose **Replace With This School’s Dates**. A class with no terms shows **Use This School’s Dates** instead.
3. Under **This Class Runs**, choose **Full Year**, **Semester 1** or **Semester 2**. A semester class keeps only that semester's terms, with the school's report numbers.
4. Choose **Save Terms**.

![The Reporting Terms tab with Salish Secondary's four terms filled in](img/reporting-terms.jpg 'The Reporting Terms tab after Replace With This School’s Dates.')

## Edit the Dates

1. On the **Reporting Terms** tab, choose **Edit Dates**.
2. Change a term's name, start date or end date. Choose **+ Add Term** to add a term, up to six, or ✕ to remove one.
3. Choose **Save Terms**. **Cancel** keeps the saved dates.

If two terms overlap, or a term ends before it starts, that term's row turns red and says why, and the terms cannot be saved until it is fixed. Terms may leave a gap between them.

## Saved Reports Stay Saved

Report comments and ratings are saved under their report number: Report 1 is Term 1, and so on. Changing a term's dates keeps them. If you remove a term that has saved reports, the editor says how many; they stay saved and come back if the class has that term again.
