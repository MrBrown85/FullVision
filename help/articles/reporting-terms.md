## Use a School's Dates

1. Open **Class Management** from the Dashboard, select the class, then open the **Reporting Terms** tab.
2. Choose the school under **School**. Its term dates are listed under the menu.
3. Choose **Replace With This School’s Dates**. A class with no terms shows **Use This School’s Dates** instead.
4. Under **This Class Runs**, choose **Full Year**, **Semester 1** or **Semester 2**. A semester class keeps only that semester's terms, with the school's report numbers.
5. Choose **Save Terms**.

![The Reporting Terms tab with Salish Secondary's four terms filled in](img/reporting-terms.jpg 'The Reporting Terms tab after Replace With This School’s Dates.')

## Edit the Dates

1. On the **Reporting Terms** tab, choose **Edit Dates**.
2. Change a term's name, start date or end date. Choose **+ Add Term** to add a term, up to six, or ✕ to remove one.
3. Choose **Save Terms**. **Cancel** keeps the saved dates.

If two terms overlap, or a term ends before it starts, the problem is stated beside **Save Terms** and the terms cannot be saved until it is fixed.

If the class has no terms and the school is not listed, choose **Quarters (4)**, **Trimesters (3)**, **Semesters (2)** or **Custom**, then adjust the dates.
