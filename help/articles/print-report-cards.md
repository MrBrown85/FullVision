## Steps

1. Open **Reporting** and choose **Print Reports**. Choose the report period in the list beside it.
2. Open **Setup** and choose a starting point: **Grades**, **Report Card**, **Interim Update** or **Conference Pack**. A class that already had a report opens with the same parts in the same order.
3. **On the Report** lists the parts that print, in print order. The switch beside a part takes it off or puts it back. To move a part, drag its icon up or down, or select the icon and use the up and down arrow keys. Choose a part's name to open its options. **Add a Part** lists the parts that are off, and **Feedback Pages** adds pages of rubrics, assessment comments or observations after the report.

![Print Reports with the Report Card setup on Cece Adeyemi's front page](img/print-report-cards.jpg 'The Report Card setup. The line above the pages counts the sheets each student needs.')

4. The pages beside the panel show what prints. A part that reaches the foot of a page carries on at the top of the next under its heading, "(continued)". The **Parent / Guardian Response** always prints at the foot of the last page.
5. On the page, **Hide** takes a part off the report. The − beside a row leaves that row off. Double-click a heading, or select it and press Enter, to rename it. In the **Assessments** part's options, **Choose Assessments** sets which assessments are listed. Leaving a row or an assessment off changes what prints, never a grade.

   The **Assessments** part's options also set how the list is laid out. **Organize By** prints **One List**, or a heading for each **Category** (with its weight and grade when **Category Grades** is on), **Month** or **Competency**. **Columns** prints the list in **One** column or **Two** side by side. **Range** lists the **Last 10**, **This Term** or the **Year to Date**. **Only Missing Work** prints just the missing pieces under the heading **Missing Work**, or "None" with today's date; it always lists every missing piece. A Letter class can also turn **Score**, **Percent** and **Proficiency Word** on or off.

   **Work Habits** prints each habit with what it means and the teacher's rating, and a line that says what Rarely, Sometimes, Usually and Consistently mean. Turn on **Student Self-Assessment** to add a circle for each word, so the student rates each habit beside the teacher. The two student reflections print as forms with a circle per row and ruled lines under each question; **Lines to Write On** sets how many.

   In a Proficiency class, every report shows a level as its proficiency, such as Proficient or Developing +, never as a number out of 4. In **Assessments** each assessment has one **Result**: its proficiency, its points such as 8 / 10, Complete or Incomplete, Missing or Excused. Work not yet assessed is left blank. The grade line is **Overall Proficiency**. A Both class adds a **Percent** column and prints the **Course Grade**. A Letter class shows **Score**, **Out Of** and %.

6. Click the **Teacher Comment** on the page to edit it. Changes are marked until they are saved. Clicking off the comment asks whether to **Save**, **Discard** or **Keep Editing**. A saved comment is saved to the Term Questionnaire. Unsaved changes never print.
7. The options of the two student reflections and the **Parent / Guardian Response** open **Edit Questions** or **Edit Wording**. The new wording is for this class only. **Reset to Original Wording** puts the original back.
8. **Changes Apply To** sets who a change is for: **Class**, or the student on the page. A part switched on or off, or a row left off, for one student is kept for that student only.
9. Choose **Letter** or **Legal**. **One Student** shows one student's pages; **Everyone** shows the whole class.
10. Choose **Print Reports**. The dialog lists the setup, the sheets and the copier settings. Set **Which Students** and **Name**, then choose **Print** to open the browser's print dialog.

Each print is saved as a copy. A list beside the report period then offers **Live** and each printed copy. A printed copy shows the reports as they went home, and **Print This Copy** prints it again.
