## Nov 24, 2026: Print or Keep a Handout

A student who opens a PDF handout on their page can now press **Print** to print every page, or **Save to Phone** to keep the whole handout as one PDF file. On an iPhone, Save to Phone opens the share sheet, which has Print, Save to Files and Google Drive. Both work in Preview as Student too. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Nov 24, 2026: Quizzes

A quiz is a new way for students to do an assessment. On the assessment form, **They Answer a Quiz** opens a Quiz card for writing the questions, the right answers and the competency each question counts toward. Students answer on their Student Page, on a phone or a Chromebook, and the teacher sees the answers by student, by question and by competency, with a suggested level for each competency. A suggested level counts nowhere until the teacher confirms it. Quizzes need the class's Student Page on. **Download All My Data** has two new sheets for quizzes; a class backup does not hold them. See [Make a Quiz](make-a-quiz).

## Nov 18, 2026: Bulletins

**Bulletins** is a new tab after Assignments: a feed of announcements and reminders for the class, posted from the laptop, from the Dashboard's Bulletins tile, or from the phone app's new fourth tab. A bulletin can hold a link, a photo or a PDF, and a reminder can stay pinned until a day and link to an assessment. Each bulletin shows how many students have seen it; select the count to see who has not. **Let Students React** lets students answer with Got It, Done or I Have a Question. On the Student Page, Bulletins is the first tab, and a class with a bulletin opens on it. Only the teacher sees who has seen a bulletin or reacted. See [Post Bulletins](post-bulletins).

## Nov 18, 2026: The Dashboard's Middle Row

Between the top bar and the student cards, the Dashboard now has one row as tall as the window allows; the student cards start below it. On the left, in a class whose students send work, the **Inbox** shows the work that has come in. **By Assessment** lists each assessment with work to assess, newest first, with **Grade Submissions** to open the grader on it. **Side by Side**, **Flip Through** and **Roll Call** show the same work as pages side by side, one student at a time, or the whole class. With nothing left to assess, the Inbox says **All Caught Up**, and a scan or files dropped on it open Add Work for the Class with them placed. In a class that does not take work, the left tile is **Recent Assessments**. On the right, one **Competencies** tile switches between **Curricular** and **Core**; select a row to see its students. To Grade in the top bar now goes to the Inbox.

## Nov 16, 2026: Reflect

The Questionnaires tab in Class Settings is now Reflect, and each item on it is a reflection. Students open reflections from the Reflect tab on their page after they sign in with their card; questionnaire slips are no longer printed. A reflection's On switch puts it in every student's Reflect tab until it is turned off, and a closing date is optional. See Answers shows what students wrote. New Reflection builds a reflection from Choice, Text, Rating, Date, Ranking, Likert and Heading questions, and Preview as a Student shows it as students will see it. A reflection built this way can be copied to another class or deleted until a student answers it.

## Nov 14, 2026: The Student Page, Redesigned

The page students open with their card now has three tabs. Assignments shows what they still have to do, under Outstanding (due today or earlier, Missing, or sent back for revision) and Upcoming, each card with its due date, your instructions, handouts and links, and Send My Work when it's on. My Learning is their report card: their grade for the year so far when Show Overall Grade is on, then every piece they've sent or got back, with its result. Tapping a piece shows your comment and each rubric row with its level and your comment on it. Reflections holds their questionnaires. Phone reports are no longer on the student page, and Done is now Log Out, which asks before it signs the student out. Preview as Student shows the new page.

## Nov 12, 2026: Students Read Proficiencies

In a class graded by Proficiency, everything a student or family reads now names the proficiency instead of a number: the Quick Report, Progress Reports, printed rubrics, phone reports and the student page. A piece of work reads like "Proficient +", and a competency or the overall grade reads like "Proficient". Nothing shows "out of 4", and a level you set by hand is the only level shown. A Both class shows the proficiency with its percent. Letter classes, and work scored in points or Complete/Incomplete, are unchanged. The gradebook and your other teacher screens keep their numbers.

## Nov 11, 2026: Import Roster

A new class's Students tab now shows **Add Your Students**: drop a class list there or choose **Choose File**, with MyEd's three steps beside it. A MyEd class list goes straight to Ready to Add with nothing to match. Any other file opens **Check How Your File Was Read**, with each column's choice above its first rows and the guesses marked **Best Guess**. Birthdays like 5/22/2012 are read the way the column shows them. A date that isn't real, a student number used twice or a row with no name is listed on its row, and never stops the import. Afterwards a panel says what was added, and **Undo This Import** takes the new students back out.

## Nov 6, 2026: Download All My Data

**Download All My Data** in the account menu saves one Excel file with every class: a Summary, a gradebook sheet per class, and sheets for rubric levels, feedback, observations, reports, questionnaires and goals.

## Nov 6, 2026: Class Results

Hover over a Class Average in the Gradebook to see the spread of the class's results. Select it, or choose **View Class Results** in the column's menu, to open **Class Results**: the results by level, criterion and competency, who is below Proficient or Missing, feedback and observations on the work, and the same assessment in your other classes. Each assessment on the Assignments page has a **Class Results** button too.

## Nov 5, 2026: Dashboard Cards

Each student card's strip is now one labelled chip per folder, coloured by the student's level; point at a chip for its full name and level. The trend arrow sits beside the grade, and "N not assessed" appears only when a competency has no scored work yet. The Curricular Competencies card has a view menu beside its title: **Class Average**, **Level Mix**, **Needs Support** and **Change**. Click a competency to sort the class by it, weakest first, with a chip above the cards to clear it, a button to record an observation for the students below, and a link into the gradebook. The Core Competencies card shows students' **Self-Assessment** this term, or the **Evidence** tagged with each. On the student page, click the email address to copy it.

## Nov 5, 2026: Class Settings

The Class Management button on the Dashboard is now **Class Settings**. It opens the same screen, with the same tabs.

## Nov 5, 2026: Help Button and Keyboard Shortcuts

A **Help** button at the top right opens a panel beside the page. It lists the help pages for that page and for Getting Started, searches them, and shows a page in the panel; **Open in Help Site** opens it in full. **Keyboard Shortcuts**, in the panel or with the ? key, lists the app's keyboard shortcuts. On a phone, Help is in the Settings sheet.

## Nov 5, 2026: Roster Import and Codes

**Import Roster** first lists the columns a roster file can hold, with **Download Template** and **Choose File**. Hovering over a code beside a student's name, such as IE, shows what it means. Screens that called a competency a standard or a tag now say competency.

## Nov 5, 2026: Demo Mode Label

In Demo Mode, a **Demo Mode** label sits at the left of the top bar. Its menu offers **Create an Account**, **Sign In** and **Reset Demo Data**.

## Nov 5, 2026: Help Pages

Help pages at fullvision.ca/help explain each task step by step, starting with [Getting Started](/help). Each page quotes the app's own button and field names, and each task page shows a picture from Demo Mode.

## Nov 5, 2026: Setting Up a First Class

With no classes yet, Class Settings shows two cards: **Create a Class** and **Try Demo Mode**. **Open Demo Mode** asks before it replaces the data on the device. A new class opens on its **Students** tab, and **Save and Add Another** keeps the Add Student form open for the next student. In the New Class wizard, **Next** stays in place when a course is ticked, and the class cannot be created without a **Class Name**.

## Nov 5, 2026: Sensitive Health Topics in Report Comments

**Generate Comment** no longer writes about sensitive health topics, such as puberty, identity, bullying, substance use and mental well-being, and gives them no line for home. Their sentences can still be chosen from **Browse Sentences**.
