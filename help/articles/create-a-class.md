## Steps

1. Choose **Create a Class**. If a class already exists, open **Class Settings** from the Dashboard and choose **New Class**.
2. **Choose Courses:** pick the **Grade**, then tick every course taught in this class. Ticking more than one makes a combined class; Humanities 8 ticks English Language Arts 8 and Social Studies 8 together. Grades 10 to 12 have a box to find a course by name. **Your Class**, beside the list, shows the class as it is made. Choose **Next**.

![Choose Courses with Grade 8 and Humanities 8 ticked, and Your Class beside the list](img/create-a-class.jpg 'Choose Courses with Humanities 8 ticked.')

3. **Name Your Class:** the **Class Name** comes from the courses. Change it if needed, and pick the **Block**, when **This Class Runs** and how **Grades Show As**.
4. **Already Set for You** lists what the class starts with. Choose **Change** beside **Competencies** to untick any you won't assess, or beside **Calculation** to change how scores are combined. How courses and folders count toward the overall is set on the Grading tab once the class is made. The **Reporting Terms** start as Grandview Heights Secondary's; choose **Change** beside them to take another school's dates, another class's, or your own.
5. Choose **Create Class**.

The new class opens on its **Students** tab. See [Add Your Students](add-your-students). A class's description and late work policy are on its Details tab in Class Settings.
