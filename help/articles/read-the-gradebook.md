## Rows and Columns

Each row is a student. The number beside the name is the student's overall grade, shown as the class's grading system sets it. In a Proficiency class it is a level from 1.0 to 4.0 with its word, such as 3.1 Proficient. A Letter class shows a letter grade and percent, and a Both class shows both. In place of a letter grade, IE means Insufficient Evidence, and No Letter Grade means the student is reported on language or IEP goals. A short code after a name is the student's BC designation; hover over it to see the designation's name.

Hover over a name to see the course grade, each category's grade and each competency's level with the number of assessments behind it. Select the name to open the student's page.

Each column is an assessment, newest on the left, with its date under the title. Points work also shows its total, such as "Out of 10". **Needs a Category** under a title means the class has categories and this assessment has none yet. Select a title to open the column's menu.

The **Class Average** row at the foot of the grid averages each column.

Hover over a Class Average to see how the class did: how many are scored, how many sit at each level, the median and range, and how many are Missing, Excused or Late. Select it, or choose **View Class Results** in the column's menu, to open **Class Results** beside the grid. It shows the same figures with the results for each rubric criterion or competency (select a number to list those students), the students **Below Proficient** with **Record an Observation** for them, who is Missing, how many have written feedback, the observations linked to the work, the same assessment in your **Other Classes**, and earlier work in the same category. Points work shows its scores as bars, a donut or dots; Complete/Incomplete work lists who is unmarked, with Mark Missing and Mark Excused. **Grade This Assessment** opens the grader. The Assignments page has a **Class Results** button on each assessment.

## What a Cell Shows

- Work scored by level or rubric shows a level from 1.0 to 4.0: the average of the competencies or criteria scored.
- Points work shows the points earned.
- Complete/Incomplete work shows ✓ for **Complete** and ✗ for **Incomplete**.
- A dash (—) means no score yet.

A cell's colour is its level. Points work takes the level its percent falls in.

## The Key

The line under the grid explains the colours and marks.

- Red is **Emerging** (1), amber **Developing** (2), green **Proficient** (3) and blue **Extending** (4).
- M is **Missing**. Hover over an M to see how missing work counts in this class.
- EXC is **Excused**. Excused work is not counted.
- L after a score is **Late**. The score still counts.
- S after a score is **Graded Separately**: a member of pair or group work graded on their own. It appears in the key only when the class has one.
- A dash (—) is **No Score**.

## Scores and Competencies Views

**Scores** shows one column per assessment. **Competencies** splits each assessment into a column for each competency it is scored on, under the assessment's title, category and date. Each cell shows that competency's level with its plus or minus: 3+ is Proficient plus, 2− is Developing minus. Points work shows the points over the total, with the percent below. A badge such as "/ 80" gives the total, and one such as "2x" gives the assessment's weight.

A code such as PC3 names one curricular competency. The letters stand for its heading in the BC curriculum and the number for its place under that heading: in Science 8, PC is Planning and Conducting. Hover over or select a code to see the competency's wording and its I Can statements.

In the Competencies view, the menu in the grid's top-left corner sets the columns: **Rubrics by Criterion** gives one column per rubric criterion, and **Rubrics by Competency** one per competency.

## Sort, Filter and Search

- **Newest First** puts the latest assessments on the left. Turn it off to put the oldest first.
- **Student** sorts by name. Each click moves to the next order: Last Name A–Z, Last Name Z–A, First Name A–Z, then First Name Z–A.
- **Overall** sorts by overall grade, lowest first. A second click puts the highest first.
- **Filters** shows a chip for each category and competency. Selected chips limit the columns to matching assessments. **Clear All** removes every filter.
- The search box limits the rows to students whose names match.
- **Export** downloads the scores as a CSV file.
