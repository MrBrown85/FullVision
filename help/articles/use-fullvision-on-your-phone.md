## Open the Phone App

1. On a phone, open fullvision.ca and sign in. The phone app opens with the class on the **Students** tab.
2. To use the full desktop layout on the phone instead, choose **Settings** (the gear at the top right), then **Switch to Desktop**. The phone keeps this choice until the next sign-out.
3. With more than one class, choose **Settings** and pick a class under **Active Class**.

![The phone app's Students tab with one student's card and the tab bar](img/use-fullvision-on-your-phone.jpg 'The Students tab. The tab bar at the foot switches between Students, Observe and Grade.')

## Students

Each student has a card with the overall level and a level for each competency. Swipe left or right to move between cards, or choose **All Students** to find a student by name. On a card, **Observe** records an observation about that student and **View Profile** opens the student's full record. **Customize Card** in **Settings** sets what each card shows.

## Observe

The class's observations are listed newest first. Choose the + button to write an observation, or **Start a sweep** to go through the class one student at a time and record a quick observation for each.

## Grade

The class's assessments are listed under **Recent** or **All**. Choose an assessment to score one student at a time. Swipe, or select a student's initials along the top, to change student. **Missing**, **Excused** and **Late** set a student's status for that assessment.
