## Open the Phone App

1. On a phone, open fullvision.ca and sign in. The phone app opens with the class on the **Students** tab.
2. To use the full desktop layout on the phone instead, choose the ⋯ at the top right, then **Switch to Desktop**. The phone keeps this choice until the next sign-out.
3. With more than one class, choose the class's name at the top of any tab and pick another class from the list.

![The phone app's Students tab with one student's card and the tab bar](img/use-fullvision-on-your-phone.jpg 'The Students tab. The tab bar at the foot switches between Students, Observe, Grade and Bulletins.')

## Students

Each student has a card with the overall level and the five newest pieces of work with their results. Swipe left or right to move between cards, or tap the student's name at the top of the card to find a student by name. On a card, **Observe** records an observation about that student and **Profile** opens the student's record, starting with what the student has said about themselves.

## Observe

The class's observations are listed newest first. Choose the + button to write an observation, or **Sweep** at the left of the row of students to go through the class one student at a time and record a quick observation for each.

## Grade

The class's assessments are listed under **Recent** or **All**. Choose an assessment to score one student at a time. Swipe, or select a student's initials along the top, to change student. **Missing**, **Excused** and **Late** set a student's status for that assessment.

## Bulletins

The class's bulletins are listed with how many students have seen each one. **Write a Bulletin** posts an announcement or a reminder, and **Post a Photo** posts a picture taken on the spot. See [Post Bulletins](post-bulletins).
