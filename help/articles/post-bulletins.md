A bulletin is a short post to every student in a class: an announcement, or a reminder that stays at the top of the feed. Students read bulletins on their Student Page and cannot post or reply. Nothing on a bulletin is a grade.

## Post a Bulletin

1. Choose **Bulletins** in the tabs at the top, after Assignments, or **Write a Bulletin** on the Dashboard's Bulletins tile. **Open Bulletins →** on the tile opens the page.
2. Select the box at the top of the page. Choose **Announcement** or **Reminder**, then write the words.
3. For a reminder, set **Stays Pinned Until** to keep it at the top until that day, and choose an assessment under **Linked To** to link it to that piece of work.
4. To add a website, choose **Add a Link**, then fill in **Link Address** and, if wanted, **Link Name (Optional)**. To add a photo or a PDF, choose **Add a File**.
5. Turn on **Let Students React** to let students answer with one tap. It is off for each new bulletin.
6. Choose **Post**.

A bulletin written with no connection shows **Not Sent Yet** and goes out when the connection returns.

## Seen By and Reactions

Each bulletin shows how many students have seen it, such as "Seen by 25 of 27 · 2 Not Seen", or **Not Seen Yet**. The count includes only the students who can open their page: a paused student, a withdrawn student and a student with no card yet are not counted. Select the count to see who has and has not seen it. The button under the list, such as "Show It Again on These 2 Phones", moves the bulletin to the newest end of the feed for the students who have not seen it.

With reactions on, students choose Got It, Done or I Have a Question, and the bulletin shows how many chose each. Only the teacher sees who has seen a bulletin and who reacted.

## Pin, Edit and Delete

The ⋯ menu on a bulletin has **Edit**, **Pin** (or **Unpin**) and **Delete**. A pinned bulletin is listed under **Pinned**, above the rest; a reminder unpins itself after its Stays Pinned Until day. Delete asks first, and deletes the bulletin with its files and every student's Seen and reaction.

## From the Phone

On the phone app, **Bulletins** is the fourth tab. **Write a Bulletin** opens the same choices in a sheet, and says which class it posts to. **Post a Photo** opens the camera for a picture of the board, with **Retake** before posting; a photo of the board can show students, so check it for faces before it is posted. Select a bulletin's Seen line to see who has seen it.

## What Students See

Bulletins needs the class's Student Page on in **Class Settings**; until then the page says so, and a bulletin can still be posted. On the Student Page, Bulletins is the first tab. A class with a bulletin opens on it, at a red New line above the ones the student has not seen; a class with none opens on Assignments. A pinned reminder also stays as a slim bar under the header, and a reminder linked to an assessment opens that assessment's card. A file's pages swipe sideways, and a link asks before it opens the website. A student sees their own reaction and nothing about classmates: no names and no counts.
