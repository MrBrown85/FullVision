## More Keys

These keys are shown as hints on the screens where they work.

- Move through the rubric list, use the highlighted rubric, close the list: ↑ and ↓, Enter, Esc (**Choose a Rubric** in the New Assessment form)
- Open the selected criterion's checklist: K (grading with a rubric)
- Add a plus or a minus to the selected criterion's level: + or − (grading with a rubric)
- Change student, or group for group work: ← and → (the scoring window)
- Save the points and move to the next student: Enter (the scoring window, on points work)
- Close the scoring window: Esc
- Open the scoring window for the selected score: Enter (Gradebook)
- Save an observation, start a new line, cancel: Enter, Shift+Enter, Esc (editing an observation)
- Choose more than one student: Shift-click (recording an observation)
- Move down a column, move across: Enter or ↓, Tab (the student table on the **Students** tab)
- Close the **Add Student** form: Esc
- Add the typed I Can statement: Enter (**Competencies** tab)
- Save the comment and open the next student, as **Approve and Next** does: Ctrl+Enter (Term Questionnaire)
- Copy the comment as plain text, as **Copy** does: Ctrl+Shift+C (Term Questionnaire)
- Put back a sentence taken out with **Remove From Comment**: Ctrl+Z (Term Questionnaire)
- Close a student's Skills or Observations panel: Esc (Term Questionnaire)
