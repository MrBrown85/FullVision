## Steps

1. Open **Observations**. The capture strip is at the top of the page.
2. Choose **+ Student** and select a student. Shift-click selects more than one. Typing @ and a name in the box also adds a student.
3. Choose a type: **Strength**, **Growth**, **Concern** or **Note**. Concerns and notes are marked **Private** and are never printed or sent home.
4. Type the observation in the box, or start from a sentence starter (below).
5. Choose **+ Add Tag** for a learning disposition, Core Competency or custom tag, and **+ Context** for the setting, such as Whole class or Small group.
6. To link the observation, choose **+ Link**, then **+ Assessment** or **+ Competency**. A linked competency can take a **Level**, which is evidence only and never changes a grade.
7. Choose **Add**, or press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line in the box.

The observation appears in the list under its date, and the strip clears for the next one.

![The capture strip with a student chosen and the starter menu open](img/record-observations.jpg 'The + Starter menu lists the most-used starters first.')

## Sentence Starters

A sentence starter is a saved opening line with its type, context and tags. The same starters are offered in every class.

1. Choose **+ Starter**, or type / in the box, then select a starter. Its text goes in the box, and it sets any type, context and tags not chosen yet.
2. Finish the sentence, then choose **Add**.

To keep the text in the box as a new starter, choose **Save as Starter**, then **Save Starter**. The observation itself is added only with **Add**.

**Edit My Starters…** at the foot of the **+ Starter** menu opens **My Sentence Starters**. Starters are added there with **Add Starter**, and edited, reordered or deleted in the list.

In the student list at the left of the Observations page, the code beside each name is the student's overall level: EMG is Emerging, DEV Developing, PRF Proficient and EXT Extending. IE, Insufficient Evidence, means no score yet.
