Grading

Grade With a Rubric, One Criterion at a Time

Tick the descriptors the student met and FullVision suggests a level for that criterion. You set the final level.

  • The Proficiency Scale with + and −, points, or letter grades for 10 to 12
  • Pair and group work, graded together or one by one
  • A comment on any criterion, and one for the whole piece

Printing

Hand Back a Graded Rubric

Print Rubrics makes a sheet for every student: the level you chose on each criterion, their overall level and room for your feedback.

  • One, two or four to a page, with cut lines
  • Black and white, ready for the photocopier
  • Blank copies to hand out before the work begins

Gradebook

Every Assignment for the Year in One Table

Each student’s overall level sits beside their scores, newest first, so a Learning Update shows the year to date. Missing work is marked Missing and is never averaged in as a zero.

BC Curriculum

The Curriculum in the Ministry’s Own Words

Pick a course when you make a class. Its competencies arrive word for word, with an I Can statement for each one, 5,029 in all.

It suits a teacher new to proficiency grading and one who has used it for years: grade in as much detail as you want.

Line by Line Tie each line of a rubric to a competency and score them one at a time. Every line counts toward its own competency.
One Mark for the Piece Or give the whole piece a single level, points or a letter grade. It still counts toward the competencies you chose.

On Your Phone

Record Observations on Your Phone

Record a Strength, Growth or Concern during class, in the lab or on a field trip. Each note is saved to the student’s profile and is there when you write their report.

Policy and Privacy

Built Around BC’s Reporting Policy

How FullVision follows the provincial reporting policy and looks after student information.

The K–12 Reporting Policy

The Proficiency Scale, descriptive feedback, Core Competency self-assessment and goals, and Learning Updates through the year.

Student Records Stay in Canada

Stored in Canada under FOIPPA and seen only by the teacher who owns the class.

No Student Accounts

Students open their page with a printed card. No student email, password or sign-up.

No AI Writing About Students

Nothing is sent to an AI service. Every comment is the teacher’s own words.

Photos Deleted on Schedule

Photos of student work are deleted on a date the teacher sets, with a reminder a week before.

Designations and Missing Work

A student’s designation shows on the class list. Missing work is never counted as a zero. Each student gets a next step.

Questions Teachers Ask

Can I try it before I sign up?

Yes. Demo Mode opens a Science 8 class with 27 sample students. Nothing you change is saved, and no account is needed.

Does it work with MyEducation BC?

Import the class list you export from MyEducation BC as it is. Students are matched by student number, so a student in two of your classes is one student.

Which grades and courses?

Grades 8 to 12, in 226 BC courses. Grades 8 and 9 use the Proficiency Scale; 10 to 12 can show letter grades and percent, or both.

Can I take my data with me?

Download a backup of any class at any time from Class Settings, and restore it later.

Does it work on an iPad or a Chromebook?

Yes, in the browser. On a phone it opens as an app you can add to your home screen, and it works offline.

Who makes FullVision?

A classroom teacher in Surrey, BC, who built it for the reports BC teachers write every term.
