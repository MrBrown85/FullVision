Student Work

Students Hand In Work and Read Your Feedback, With No Accounts

Each student gets a printed card with a code. It opens their own page in a browser: no email, no password, nothing to install.

  1. Students Send Their WorkStudents photograph their page, or upload a PDF, from their own page.
  2. You Assess ItTick, circle and leave notes on the page, then score the rubric beside it.
  3. Students Read Your FeedbackStudents read your notes, say whether the feedback made sense, and ask you a question.
BulletinsNew Announcements and reminders are the first tab on each student’s page. Post from your laptop or your phone, and see who has seen each one.
ReflectNew Self-assessments and goals appear in each student’s Reflect tab, with no slips to print. Their words are there when you write their report.

Paper WorkNew

Scan the Class’s Pile. Each Student Gets Their Own Pages.

Scan a stack of paper on the school photocopier and open the PDF in Add Work for the Class. FullVision splits it into each student’s pages and files them under the assessment.

  • A pile in class-list order names itself; or type two letters of a name
  • Blank backs are left out, and a page fed in upside down turns with one click
  • Nothing reads the handwriting. The scan is split on your computer, and only each student’s pages upload.

Reports

Write Report Comments From Your Own Records

While you write a comment, the student’s grades, your observations and their self-assessment are on the same screen. Every report fills in from your gradebook, and you choose what prints.

  • A Comment Bank of written sentences, filled in from each student’s grades and work habits
  • Generate drafts a comment, using a sentence only when the student’s record supports it
  • Start from Grades, Report Card, Interim Update or Conference Pack, then switch parts on or off
  • Black and white, on Letter or Legal

Questions Teachers Ask

Can I try it before I sign up?

Yes. Demo Mode opens a Science 8 class with 27 sample students. Nothing you change is saved, and no account is needed.

Does it work with MyEducation BC?

Import the class list you export from MyEducation BC as it is. Students are matched by student number, so a student in two of your classes is one student.

Which grades and courses?

Grades 8 to 12, in 226 BC courses. Grades 8 and 9 use the Proficiency Scale; 10 to 12 can show letter grades and percent, or both.

Can I take my data with me?

Download a backup of any class at any time from Class Settings, and restore it later.

Does it work on an iPad or a Chromebook?

Yes, in the browser. On a phone it opens as an app you can add to your home screen, and it works offline.

Who makes FullVision?

A classroom teacher in Surrey, BC, who built it for the reports BC teachers write every term.
