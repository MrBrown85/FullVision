Assignments and Rubrics

Score Every Criterion Against Clear Success Criteria

Make an assignment, attach a rubric, and score each student criterion by criterion. On an Advanced Rubric, tick what the student showed and FullVision suggests the proficiency. You can always pick a different one.

  • Create assignments with dates, a category, and the curricular and Core Competencies they assess.
  • Score by proficiency with + and −, points, complete or incomplete, or DIBELS 8.
  • Advanced Rubrics hold a checklist in each level. The suggested level is the highest one with every box ticked.
  • Set Everyone To a level, then change the few who differ.
  • Comment on any criterion, and add an overall comment for each student.
  • Mark work Late, Excused or Missing.

Every Score in One Grid

  • Keep rubrics in a Rubric Bank and print graded copies to hand back.
  • Add one assignment to every block that teaches the course. Edits stay linked.

Custom Reports

Design Your Own Report Card

Click or drag blocks onto the page. Each block fills itself from your gradebook for every student, so there is nothing to type.

  • Grades: proficiency by competency, assignment grades, missing work, attendance and progress in one area.
  • Comments and evidence: your teacher comment and next steps.
  • Student voice: self-assessment and goal, and reflections on learning and habits.
  • A proficiency legend and a parent or guardian response strip.
  • Start from Report Card, Interim Update or Conference Pack, then Save as Template.
  • Letter or Legal, front only or front and back, black or colour.

Quick Report

Print the Whole Class at Once

  • A grade sheet for every student with each assignment, a category summary and the term grade.
  • Add feedback pages for rubrics, assignment comments, observations and the term comment.
  • One student or everyone, in one print job.
  • Sent to Phones puts the report on each student's phone instead of paper.

Rubric Feedback

Hand Back a Graded Rubric to Every Student

  • Completed rubrics, one per student, with their scores marked.
  • Or a blank rubric with no names or scores, to hand out before the task.
  • Print for all scored students, the whole class, or the students you pick.
  • One, two or four rubrics per page: full page, half sheets or quarter sheets.
  • Black and white, ready for the photocopier.

Student Voice

Hear From Students Without Student Accounts

Hand out printed QR slips. Students scan with their phones, or type the code on the slip, and answer the questions you wrote. Their answers go straight to their Learner Profile.

  • About Me, goals and reflections in the student's own words.
  • Questionnaires you write, from templates or from scratch.
  • Name pronunciation, pronouns and home languages.
  • Private notes from the student to you.
  • Send progress reports to students' phones, and collect one reply from each.

Observations

Write Down What You See

  • Mark each note as a Strength, Growth, Concern or Note.
  • Type @name to pick a student, and tag the note to a competency or disposition.
  • Add context, like Independent or Small Group, and save your own starters.
  • Keep private notes for yourself.
  • Filter by student, tag, type, date or assessment.

Class Dashboard

Know Who Needs You Today

  • Class average and how many students are Extending, Proficient and Developing.
  • Needs Attention lists students below Proficient, not observed in 3+ weeks, with no strength recorded, or with missing work. You set the rule.
  • Proficiency for each curricular competency, and how many assessments cover each Core Competency.
  • Missing and late work, and upcoming birthdays.

Student Page

One Student, the Whole Picture

  • Proficiency for each competency, with every score from oldest to newest.
  • Select a competency to see the work and observations behind it.
  • Assessments, observations, a grade breakdown by category and the Learner Profile.
  • Ready for a parent meeting without extra prep.
